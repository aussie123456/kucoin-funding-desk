Leveraged tokens: distance to a forced rebalance

Gate resets a token to its target leverage the moment real leverage reaches a limit, and checks every token daily at 16:00 UTC (00:00 UTC+8), when it resets any whose underlying has moved more than 1%. "Reset trade" is what the fund would buy (+) or sell (−) in the underlying if reset now, as a share of its net asset value. Click a row for the price levels.
